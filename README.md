## What an OKX referral code actually pays

OKX runs two separate programs, and the difference explains almost every confusing number you'll see on code-listing pages.

The **affiliate program** pays the person whose code you use a commission of up to 50% of the trading fees your account generates, settled in USDT. Affiliates decide how much of that commission to pass back to you, and that decision is what becomes "15%" or "20%" or "30%" on a landing page. It is not an OKX discount. It is one person choosing to hand over part of their cut, so the rate varies by code and can change whenever the affiliate wants.

The **referral program** is the plain version for ordinary users: any verified primary account can invite people without applying, and the inviter gets a fixed or randomized crypto reward per invitee who completes the tasks, capped per campaign cycle. Referral rewards are locked for 7 days. Affiliate users earn affiliate rewards only and cannot double-dip on referral rewards.

Neither program's public documentation states a rate for a specific code, which is the honest reason nobody can tell you in advance what a given code returns. The registration page is the only place the number is authoritative.

## Four things to check before you type a code

### 1. The 30-day device and cache binding

If the person registering clicked someone else's invite link, or downloaded the app from a coded link, within the last 30 days, that code may already be tied to the device. Browser cache follows you across QR scans and download links, which is how a new code ends up refusing to enter. Register in a private or incognito window, and confirm the code box shows the right code before hitting submit. This is the single most common way people lose a code they intended to use, and it cannot be fixed afterward.

### 2. One account, one identity, advanced verification

The email address, phone number, and government ID you use must not have appeared on OKX before. Duplicate accounts, borrowed identity documents, and device farms void rewards across every linked account. Basic verification is not enough for referral or welcome rewards; you need the advanced tier, which means photographing your ID and completing a face check.

### 3. The 14-day task window

Trading tasks must be completed within 14 days of sign-up. Task types and thresholds vary by user, so the referral page in your own account is the authoritative list, not a screenshot from someone else's signup. Claim each reward inside its stated period, and note that claiming happens in the OKX app only, not the web interface. The app needs to be version 6.66.0 or higher. After claiming, rewards appear under My Rewards.

### 4. What counts as a deposit, and what doesn't

Eligible funding means fiat deposits and on-chain transfers from a wallet you control. Transfers from other OKX users, crypto red packets, and promotional credits don't count. Deposits are also measured net: qualifying withdrawals are subtracted, and moving funds to another OKX user or into OKX Pay lowers the balance, which can drop you below a task threshold right before verification.

Some pairs are excluded from task volume entirely: USDC/EUR, USDT/USDC, and USDC/USDG, along with zero-markup conversions such as USDC/USD and any zero-fee stablecoin pair. One more caution: trades paid for with rebate cards, contract vouchers, USDC fee waivers, or welcome bonuses generally don't register as valid volume for campaign purposes. Fund the trade with your own balance if a threshold matters.

## After the reward lands, three more gates

**Two different waiting periods.** The 7-day lock applies to inviter-side referral rewards. Invitee crypto rewards can be traded immediately but carry a withdrawal wait of up to 45 days, with the release date listed under Rewards > My Rewards. People conflate these constantly; they are separate rules.

**The holding freeze.** Crypto rewards come with an equivalent-value freeze calculated at the live price. Sell the reward coin and the price then rises, and the frozen amount can exceed what you received, briefly tying up your own funds. OKX's terms describe this as expected behavior rather than a bug.

**The 48-hour rule.** Rewards are normally distributed as soon as a task completes. If nothing shows up 48 hours after the qualifying action, contact support rather than waiting it out.

## FAQ

**Can I change my referrer after registering?** No. The relationship is created at registration only. OKX's FAQ states plainly that once registration or identity verification is complete, it cannot be changed.

**I already have an account. Can I add a code now?** No. The field only functions during sign-up. To check whether a referral relationship exists at all, open Menu > Account settings > Profile > Referral rewards in the app; if that entry isn't there, no relationship was established.

**I entered the wrong code. What now?** Fix it before you submit by reloading the sign-up page in a private window and starting from the invite link. After you've registered and verified, opening a second account is a duplicate-account risk that can void rewards on both.

**Is the $10,000 mystery box still available?** That was an older referral rule. The support article describing it now redirects to the current FAQ, which describes rewards as fixed crypto amounts or randomized amounts up to a stated cap. Don't pick a code on the promise of a five-figure box.

**When does the fee rebate arrive?** OKX documents welcome reward timing, typically immediate on task completion with a 48-hour escalation path, but the fee share is paid by the affiliate out of their own commission. The schedule is theirs, not OKX's. Ask before you route volume through the account.

**Does creating an OKX Wallet activate the code?** No. The self-custody wallet is a separate product from the exchange, and setting it up has no effect on an exchange referral.
